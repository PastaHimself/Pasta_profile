02 / What I do

How I put packs together.

A good addon needs the code, models, and UI to feel like they belong in the same game. I usually handle every part of the stack myself.

01 / logic @minecraft/server

Scripting & mechanics

Writing custom gameplay loops, server utilities, economy systems, and custom mob behaviors that hold up on live multiplayer servers.

02 / visuals Shaders & JSON UI

Visuals & interfaces

Designing custom HUDs, JSON UI menus, shaders, and color palettes so menus and effects look clean and intentional.

03 / 3D & art Blockbench

Models & animations

Modeling entities, custom items, animations, and pixel textures in Blockbench when an idea needs custom art to work.

04 / release Regolith & testing

Playtesting & shipping

Tracking down edge-case bugs, optimizing performance, and packaging clean `.mcaddon` and `.mcpack` files ready for players to drop in.

03 / Workflow

From rough test to public release.

I like taking weird ideas and figuring out how to actually build them inside Bedrock’s limits—testing, breaking things, and refining until it plays smoothly.

Pasta · @PastaHimself

Built for actual players.

Whether it’s a server utility or a horror pack, I care most about how it feels in-game. If a menu is annoying to use or a mechanic lags a realm, I rework it until it’s right.
