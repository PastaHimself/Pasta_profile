The person
behind the packs.

Most of what I make starts as a small “what if” idea. I write the logic, build the models in Blockbench, and work on the visuals until the pack feels good to play.

If a menu is annoying to use or a mechanic lags a realm, I rework it.

Gameplay & server systems

Custom mob behaviors, economy systems, and multiplayer utilities with the Bedrock Script API.

Art & interfaces
